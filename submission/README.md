## Remaining submission steps

The supplied PDF permits any 2 or 3 tasks; all four are available. Follow the official form's task-count fields. Review and understand the projects, record/post the chosen video explanations with their GitHub repository links, share internship status tagging the correct CodeAlpha page, and submit through the official form supplied in the program's WhatsApp group or email. No form URL or deadline was supplied here. The posts, recordings and official form have not been completed for you.

Descriptions were added to each project README. GitHub's separate About field could not be edited through the blocked API; PROJECT_DESCRIPTIONS.txt contains text that can be pasted into that field.

The ZIP excludes this hosted download folder from its own contents to avoid nesting copies of the archive. It includes the verified source commits listed in its repository manifest. Individual notebooks, reports and datasets are also available in each project's main repository.
